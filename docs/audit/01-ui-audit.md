## Summary

The app has a recognisable look (a space backdrop, a violet-to-cyan gradient for primary buttons, glassy cards), and the published templates share their chapter layout and narration dock well. `chapterLayout.ts` and `narrationDock.ts` handle reduced motion, focus rings and approved type sizes properly, and the other templates should follow them. The weak points:

1. **Readability on published sites.** In Space, Lunar, Airlock and the case-study layout, narration text starts at 25% white (2.0:1 contrast), or `#d4d4d4` on white (1.5:1) in the light case study. It only brightens while audio plays. A visitor who taps "Continue without sound", or who opens a chapter that has no audio, sees unreadable body text for the whole visit. Voyage already handles this correctly.
2. **The app chrome leaks into the preview.** The global theme toggle and user menu are fixed on top of every template preview, where they collide with the Showcase header and the Lunar globe button. Published sites do not have them, so the preview is not faithful.
3. **Keyboard focus is barely visible.** 17 inputs and selects use `focus:outline-none` and change only to a 1 px violet border (2.2:1 against the resting border). Only the auth pages and the user menu define `focus-visible` rings.
4. **No design tokens.** Only `--background` and `--foreground` exist. The rest is hard-coded Tailwind colours, 21 different arbitrary glow shadows, 5 radii, 3 disabled opacities, two competing primary-button styles, and H1 sizes from 24 px to 60 px across app pages.
5. **Motion.** The space backdrop (stars, meteors, asteroids, orbs), the loading bar, the chunk carousel, the Space particle canvas and the Lunar three.js scene all ignore `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. **Branding.** The case-study "Company" section is hard-coded WarpDrive marketing copy and is injected into every case study. Published sites use a `warpdrive-` hostname. If these sites are ever used for Scotty implementations, both conflict with decision D-011 (23 Oct 2026). This needs the owner's call.

## Quick wins (each under about an hour)

1. **UI-01**: on "Continue without sound" and for chapters without audio, render words at full colour (Voyage's `[data-audio]` rule). This has the biggest visible impact.
2. **UI-02**: hide ThemeToggle and UserMenu on `/projects/[id]/preview` and `case-study-preview`.
3. **UI-03**: `font-size:min(var(--lunar-title-m),15vw); overflow-wrap:anywhere` on the Lunar hero title.
4. **UI-08**: one `prefers-reduced-motion` block in `globals.css` for the backdrop and loading bar.
5. **UI-06**: `inert` on inactive chunk cards.
6. **UI-11, UI-12**: replace `indigo-200/50` and `/40` with `/60`, and add dark variants to Settings text.
7. **UI-21**: add `color-scheme` to `:root` and `.dark`.
8. **UI-17**: autofocus and `aria-modal` on the narration gate.
9. **UI-18**: lighter card padding on `/new` at phone width, a full-width number input and a back link.
10. **UI-27**: pin the Airlock video URL to a commit SHA.
